**Where technology meets the real work: essays on AI, marketing, data, and productivity, from someone who tries everything before writing about it.**

I'm Orlando O'Neill, a marketer with an engineering background. I spent the first chunk of my career as an engineer at IBM, writing test automation scripts, digging into server hardware, and earning three US patents.

Then I pivoted, through a Kellogg MBA and consulting at BCG, into marketing, where I've spent most of the last 15 years. I've worked at Microsoft, a Boston apparel startup, a B2B marketing and analytics consultancy, and Automattic. Now I'm a senior product marketing manager for Alexa at Amazon.

That combination gives me an unusual vantage point: I understand how technology works, and I spend my days figuring out how to actually use it to get things done.

Right now, most of my curiosity is pointed at AI: specifically, how non-technical people can move beyond occasional chatbot use and build something more systematic. Since August 2025, I've gone from using AI sporadically to managing a team of custom agents that I collaborate with daily. I'm writing about that journey as I go, because I find I understand things better when I try to explain them.

At work, the framework I developed for managing those agents is now used in multiple Amazon divisions. Outside work, I build my own apps for working with agents, and I'm open sourcing them. The first is [Corillo](https://oneillo.com/posts/corillo-collaborative-markdown-app/), a Markdown editor for refining documents with your AI agents.

I live in the Seattle region with my wife and daughter, and I spend much of my free time reading.

If you're curious about technology and like practical over theoretical, you'll find something here.
