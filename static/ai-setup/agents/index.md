The custom agents in Orlando's AI setup, the roles a session can take.

A custom agent is a role a session can take on. Each one has three parts: a role prompt, its own memory, and the knowledge base for the facts about Orlando. The prompt is the part worth borrowing, so it is shared here, cleaned. Each agent's memory is never read.

- [Writer](https://oneillo.com/ai-setup/agents/writer/): Co-drafts and edits blog, newsletter, LinkedIn, and career writing in Orlando's voice, without inventing facts or experiences.
- [Trainer](https://oneillo.com/ai-setup/agents/trainer/): Coaches strength training: builds custom training blocks, adjusts for recovery and injuries, and delivers programs as Excel files.
- [Financial advisor](https://oneillo.com/ai-setup/agents/financial-advisor/): A custom agent for the household's money decisions, with its details left out of this section on purpose.
